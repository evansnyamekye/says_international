Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Says International School collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information shared through the website.

Information we may collect

  • Name, phone number, email address, and message details submitted through contact or enquiry forms.
  • Application information provided by parents, guardians, or applicants through admission forms.
  • Email addresses submitted for newsletters or school updates.
  • Basic technical information such as browser type, pages visited, or device data where analytics or embedded tools are used.

How we use the information

We use information collected through the website to respond to enquiries, process applications, send requested updates, improve the website experience, and communicate important school information with parents, guardians, students, and visitors.

Sharing of information

Says International School does not sell personal information. Information may be shared only where necessary to operate the website, process school-related requests, comply with legal obligations, or work with trusted service providers who support our website or communications.

Data protection and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. Information is kept only for as long as needed for the purpose for which it was collected or as required by law or school administrative needs.

Cookies and third-party services

The website may use cookies or third-party tools such as social media embeds, analytics, maps, or video services. These services may collect data according to their own privacy policies. Visitors can manage cookie settings through their browser where applicable.

Your choices

You may contact the school to request correction of inaccurate information, ask questions about how your information is used, or request removal from email updates where appropriate.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the handling of personal data, please contact Says International School at info@saysinternationalschool.com or call +233 30 294 7154.
