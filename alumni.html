How alumni stay involved

A cleaner structure for the three things this page should do well

The alumni page now follows the same modern theme logic as academics: clear hierarchy, strong cards, readable sections, and content that belongs to SIS.

Reconnect

Stay close to your classmates and year groups

Use the page as a familiar return point for alumni conversations, milestone sharing, and reunion planning across graduating sets.

Support

Guide learners who are still on the journey

Mentor younger students, speak at school events, and help current learners see what life after SIS can look like.

Return

Come back for moments that still matter

Homecoming days, outreach drives, and campus celebrations give alumni a natural path back into the life of the school.

Upcoming alumni calendar

Three kinds of events that fit the SIS alumni story

Instead of generic blocks, the page now highlights events in the same theme language used across the better pages on the site.

Homecoming

Alumni breakfast and campus walk

Reconnect with old classmates, meet current students, and return to the spaces where your SIS journey began.

Mentorship

Career stories and student guidance panel

Share lessons from work, study, and life after school with pupils who need practical encouragement from people who once sat where they sit now.

Giving Back

Community impact and school support day

Join outreach activities, contribute to a project, and stay part of a school story that still benefits from alumni involvement.

Alumni story

The page should look like SIS, but speak directly to former students.

The aim here is not to create a completely different visual language. It is to keep the same modern theme discipline used on academics while making the message, cards, and calls to action clearly alumni-focused.

Familiar tone

The page reads like the school site, not like a disconnected landing page.

Clear next step

Alumni can immediately see how to reconnect, attend an event, or reach the school.

Better visual balance

The empty opening space is gone and the page now carries strong rhythm from top to bottom.

What this page should communicate

SIS alumni are still part of the school community, not just visitors to it.

The page now presents alumni as mentors, supporters, returning guests, and active members of a continuing SIS network.
