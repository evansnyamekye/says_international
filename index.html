Why Choose Says?

Says International School offers a safe, nurturing environment where children are known, supported and challenged. Our experienced teachers deliver a high quality, child centred curriculum. We offer two distinct curriculum tracks so families can choose the best fit: the internationally recognised Oxford International Curriculum, and the Ghana Education Service (GES) curriculum for strong local alignment. Each track provides rigorous academics, creativity, character formation and practical skills to prepare pupils for local and global opportunities.

Families choose Says because we combine modern facilities and moderate class sizes with excellent pastoral care and spiritual grounding. Students benefit from targeted individual support, rich extracurricular opportunities (Says got Talent, iWise, sports, arts, STEM) and a close community of parents, staff and alumni that actively support future pathways.

Self Development

At Says International School, self-development is at the heart of our educational philosophy. We believe that every student possesses unique talents and potential that must be nurtured and cultivated. Our comprehensive programs encourage students to discover their strengths, overcome challenges, and build confidence in their abilities.

Through a blend of academic excellence, character education, and practical experiences, we empower our learners to become independent thinkers and responsible leaders. Our dedicated educators provide mentorship and guidance, helping students set meaningful goals and develop the resilience needed to achieve them. We foster an environment where curiosity is celebrated, creativity is encouraged, and personal growth is recognized as a lifelong journey.

Spirituality

Says International School honors spiritual development as part of a balanced education. We believe in God, the finisher of all things, and seek to nurture student's moral character, compassion, and sense of purpose.

Through values-based assemblies, quiet reflection, and respectful dialogue, we converge as management and staff to worship our Lord and Master Jesus Christ and support students of all backgrounds to grow spiritually, develop integrity, and serve their community with empathy and responsibility

Alumni

The Says International School Alumni Association is proud to introduce Says Alumni Connect, a dedicated platform that brings former students together to celebrate shared memories, support one another, and build a stronger SIS community. Says Alumni Connect makes it simple to reconnect with former classmates, find mentors, post opportunities, and collaborate on initiatives that benefit both alumni and current students.

Our alumni network is focused on lifelong relationships and future development. By joining Says Alumni Connect, former students gain access to professional networking, mentoring programs, events, and fundraising efforts that help advance careers and support the school's growth. We invite all alumni to register, participate, and help shape the future of Says International School.
