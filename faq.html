Frequently Asked Questions

Quick Help

Search The Questions

Type a keyword such as admissions, fees, curriculum, PTA, or security to quickly find the answer you need.

Showing all 10 questions

Admissions

What is the admission procedure at SIS?

Parents begin by submitting an enquiry or visiting the school, after which the child may be invited for an age-appropriate assessment and placement discussion before admission is confirmed.

Admissions

What is the fee policy/structure?

Fee details are available from the admissions office, and parents are guided through payment expectations, deadlines, and any approved instalment arrangements during enrolment.

Academics

What curriculum will SIS follow?

SIS follows the Oxford International and GES curriculum to give students strong academic foundations, practical skills, and global learning exposure.

School Day

What will be the school timings?

Regular school hours are from 7:30 am to 3:00 pm, with parents informed ahead of time whenever special programmes or events affect the normal schedule.

Wellbeing

Will the school authorities be taking adequate measures to ensure hygiene in the school?

Yes. SIS maintains strict cleaning routines, monitored washroom and classroom hygiene, and regular supervision to help keep the learning environment clean, healthy, and safe.

Wellbeing

What is the kind of security offered to the students?

The school uses controlled access, CCTV monitoring, staff supervision, and structured movement routines so that students remain secure throughout the school day.

Academics

How many terms will the school have?

The academic year runs in three terms in line with the approved GES school calendar.

Parents

How often will there be PTAs?

PTA meetings are scheduled during the academic year, and parents receive notice in advance with the meeting date, time, and purpose.

Parents

Other than a PTA, when can a parent/guidance interact with the teacher?

Parents may meet teachers whenever necessary by arranging an appointment through the school so that discussions are timely, private, and productive.

Academics

How many students will be there per division?

SIS aims to maintain manageable class sizes of about 30 students to support effective teaching, supervision, and individual attention.

Purposeful Learning

We build strong learners through structured teaching, guided exploration, and consistent expectations that help children grow with confidence and character.

Care And Discipline

Respect, discipline, integrity, and care shape how we teach, guide behaviour, and create a stable environment where every child can succeed.

Progress That Shows

We focus on measurable academic progress, strong routines, and close teacher follow-up so families can clearly see growth over time.

Admissions Guidance

From first enquiry to placement and enrolment, families receive clear guidance so the admissions process stays simple, fast, and well organised.

Student Growth

Students are developed academically, socially, and practically through classroom work, activities, leadership experiences, and character formation.

Home-School Partnership

Regular updates, accessible staff communication, and responsive academic feedback help parents stay involved in each child’s progress.
