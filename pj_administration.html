Administration and School Services

Our administration team keeps school life organised, welcoming, and responsive so pupils can learn well and families stay informed with confidence.

Home >Administration and School Services

A calm, organised system behind every school day

The Administration and School Services team supports the daily life of Says International School by coordinating communication, admissions, records, pupil welfare, and the practical systems that keep learning on track.

Families should experience a school that is warm, clear, and dependable. From the first enquiry to the final bell, our team works to make every interaction professional, timely, and centred on the needs of children.

We combine strong leadership, responsive communication, and careful student support so pupils can learn confidently in a safe and orderly environment.

Parent-first Clear guidance, dependable updates, and respectful service for every family.
School-wide support Admissions, records, welfare, communication, and operational coordination in one connected team.

Core administration services

Admissions and Enquiries

We guide parents through applications, class placement, and onboarding so every new pupil joins the school with clarity and confidence.

Parent Communication

Notices, reminders, academic updates, and school guidance are shared in a timely way to keep families fully informed.

Student Records

Attendance, assessment records, and essential documentation are managed carefully to support continuity and accountability.

Pupil Welfare and Support

We work with teachers and families to maintain a safe, orderly, and encouraging environment where children can flourish.

How we support families

  • Prompt response to enquiries and admissions questions.
  • Consistent coordination between administration, teachers, and school leadership.
  • Clear school routines that help pupils develop responsibility and discipline.
  • Welcoming service for both returning and first-time parents.
