Welcome to Pre-School

Where your child's learning journey begins

Our Pre-School gives children a warm, structured introduction to school life – building early language, numeracy, social and motor skills through guided play, songs, stories and hands-on activities. Every day is designed to help young learners feel safe, curious and excited to learn.

Ages 3 – 5 Small class sizes Monday – Friday Qualified early-years staff

Why families choose our Pre-School

A nurturing first step into school life, built around how young children actually learn.

Play-Based Learning

Learning through play

Structured early-literacy and numeracy activities are blended with purposeful play, helping every child build confidence while they learn.

Our Caregivers

Warm, qualified staff

Each classroom is led by trained early-years educators who nurture every child's social, emotional and physical development.

Safety First

A safe, caring environment

Secure classrooms, child-friendly facilities and close supervision give parents peace of mind throughout the school day.
