SAYS Outreach Support

This page is dedicated to helping the school community support the needy through meaningful giving. People may contribute cash, new clothing, or any other valuable items that can bring comfort and hope to those in need.

At SAYS, outreach support is about compassion in action. Every gift, whether small or significant, can make a difference in the lives of families and children who need support.

Ways to contribute

  • Cash donations to support food, shelter, medical care, and school essentials
  • New clothes, shoes, and blankets for children and families
  • Books, school materials, and other useful items
  • Any item of value that can be shared with those in need

How the support helps

  • Provides immediate relief to vulnerable people
  • Encourages generosity and community care
  • Creates a lasting impact through practical support
  • Shows that every act of kindness can transform lives
