Showing up for our community

SAYS outreach brings practical support, encouragement and fellowship to children and families. Every visit is a chance to listen, share the Gospel and remind them of how Jesus loved His Children.

We welcome support in the form of donations, new clothing, fairly used clothes, shoes, books, school supplies and other useful items.
