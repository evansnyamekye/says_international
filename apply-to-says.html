Entrollment Requirement

1. You will be examined after a successful interview.

2. The Examination will be in four (4) parts:
• English
• Mathematics
• General Science
• Social Studies

3. Purchase the application form, complete and submit it to the admission office.
4. Please attach to the completed forms your child

• Last two Terminal Reports / Transcript or Testimonial from the last school.
• Known examination results
• Two (2) passport size photographs
• General Science.
• Photocopy of birth certificate or passport.
• Medical Report in the school's format.

The Application Process

1

Start Online/In-Person Submission

Start by submitting your application either online or in person at the school’s admissions office – whichever is more convenient for you.

2

Submit The Form

Complete the application by filling out the form thoroughly and submitting it either online or at the admissions desk.

3

Review The Submission

Your application will be reviewed by our admissions committee to ensure all requirements are met.

4

Gather Necessary Documents

Collect all required documents, including medical records, identification, and any other materials specified in the application guidelines.

5

Interviewing Process

The interviewing process allows us to learn more about your ward goals. You will have the opportunity to ask questions and share your interests and aspirations.

6

Last Decision

The final decision will be communicated to you via email or postal mail within a few weeks of your application submission.

Things To Know First

Says International School offers education at the Lower, Upper, and Junior High School levels. The Common Application is required for students applying to any of SIS academic classes.

You will need :

  • Optional: Contact information for your previous school's administrator or counselor who can provide your academic terminal report.
  • Optional: contact information for one or two teachers from your previous school who can provide a recommendation letter.
  • Completed application form and a non-refundable application fee. parents with more than two wards are entitled to a fee waiver.

When To Apply?

The application process for SIS is open year-round, but we encourage you to apply as early as possible to secure a spot for your ward. Below are the application deadlines and decision notification dates for 2026 and beyond:

Application Type Deadline Decision Notification
Early Admission June 30 July 15
Regular Admission August 15 August 31
Late Admission September 1 September 15
