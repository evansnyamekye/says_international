Why Choose Says?

Says International School gives children a structured and encouraging environment where UCMAS lessons strengthen concentration, speed, and confidence in mathematics.

Our school is known for strong teaching, good discipline, and a caring partnership with families that helps every learner grow academically and personally.

Self Development

Through UCMAS training, pupils develop patience, focus, and independent thinking that support success across all subjects.

These habits help children become more confident learners both inside the classroom and at home.

Spirituality

We support the whole child by combining academic excellence with values such as respect, honesty, and responsibility.

This balanced approach helps learners build strong character alongside strong performance.

Alumni

SIS alumni continue to inspire younger learners by sharing their stories, mentoring pupils, and supporting school activities.

Their achievements reflect the strong foundation built at Says International School.
