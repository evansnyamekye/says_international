Welcome to Nursery Two

Growing independence through guided play

Nursery Two builds on early foundations with more structured group activities, introducing sharing, turn-taking and the first steps in early numeracy and literacy, all through purposeful, engaging play.

Ages 3 – 4 Small class sizes Monday – Friday Qualified early-years staff

Why families choose our Nursery Two

A supportive, age-appropriate classroom experience built around how our pupils learn best.

Social Skills & Sharing

Learning together

Group activities teach pupils to share, take turns and cooperate with their classmates.

Early Numeracy & Literacy

First building blocks

Playful introductions to letters, numbers, shapes and colours prepare pupils for Kindergarten.

Creative Play

Imagination at work

Art, music and imaginative play give pupils fun, hands-on ways to express themselves.
